Table~\ref{tab:refinement} records every refinement sequence. The procedure yields collision-free solutions in eight of twelve cases, with a median cumulative NLP time of $0.485$~s. Cases 02, 08, 11, 12 reach the $10M$-point cap without a collision-free solution. Refinement can resolve a particular sampled collision, but the next optimization may move the trajectory and produce another inter-point overlap. Consequently, increasing the node count does not ensure monotone improvement in collision-frame count. The table retains these nonmonotone outcomes and charges every attempted solve to the same case. BME--OBCA instead constrains the intervening geometry in its single selected-mesh solve.
