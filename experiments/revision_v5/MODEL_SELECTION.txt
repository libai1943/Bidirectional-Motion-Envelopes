The complete 72-run tangent-constrained diagnostic was replaced after redundant tangent constraints caused severe dual divergence. The next 72-run model, without those constraints, achieved 12/12 accepted runs at beta=0.5, 0.6 and 0.7. A further trial removing the redundant nonnegative lower bound of the angular expression reduced successful terminations. That equivalent-expression trial is preserved under diagnostic-angle-upper-only. The validated previous model is retained in the final experiment set. No individual result was substituted across model versions. All comparison methods use this retained model and common solver settings.
